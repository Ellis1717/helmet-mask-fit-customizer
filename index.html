Photo-based calibration (manual landmarks)

Take one straight-on front photo and one true side-profile photo. Hold a ruler or another known-length reference in the same plane as the head to minimize perspective errors. In each image, click the two ends of your reference, then the two ends of your head span. Front = widest head width; side = front-to-back depth.
